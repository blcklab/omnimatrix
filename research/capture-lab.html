OmniMatrix — M8 Capture Lab

Research-only, completely local photo-analysis interface. M8 adds experimental no-click detection for one isolated, near-frontal HYBRID symbol on a light background. Manual four-point registration remains available for harder photographs. No upload, server-side photo processing, or production scanning claims. Camera photos remain in your browser.

Choose a photograph, then use Auto-detect or mark the reference points.
Mark four perimeter points in order (clockwise in *symbol* axes): 1. primary notch at +X (wide white gap), 2. +Y (quarter-turn clockwise from primary), 3. −X, 4. −Y. The secondary narrow notch is around +120°. If the image is mirrored, do not reverse the click order: the registration routine will explicitly reject reversed winding; un-mirror the image first. The point must be at the CENTER of the black outer rim (radius S+9), not at the outermost white edge.
Optional center mark: not set

Automatic detection is experimental: it supports one roughly circular, near-frontal code on a light quiet zone, and it requires a CRC-valid optical header. Use the manual four-point workflow for difficult photos. For manual decoding, four approximate rim marks are required. The lab can then recover S/palette from a CRC-valid optical header and refine the perspective transform using reserved calibration, header and alignment features. It does not find symbols automatically in arbitrary photos. Data cells are used only for post-refinement evaluation, never to tune alignment. Use Measure known research pattern only for old M1 fixtures. To recover M5/M6 encoded bytes, use Decode real payload (M7); its result is independently validated by RS and CRC-32. M7 calibration controls are optional experiments; try both and report failures as well as successes. No method is proven optimal for phone photographs. No production camera-decoding reliability has been established.
